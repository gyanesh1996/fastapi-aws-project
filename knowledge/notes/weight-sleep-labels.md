# Weight, sleep, screen time, food labels

## weight-when-people-gain
source: ICMR-NIN Dietary Guidelines for Indians 2024, Guideline 9 "Points to register", PDF p. 85
There is a tendency to gain weight mostly around 20 years of age; and among women after childbirth because of less physical activity and excess calorie intake.

## weight-slow-and-steady
source: ICMR-NIN Dietary Guidelines for Indians 2024, Guideline 9 "Points to register", PDF p. 85
Regular yoga and physical activity are essential. Slow and steady reduction in body weight is advisable. Extreme approaches for weight loss may lead to health hazards.

## weight-what-to-cut
source: ICMR-NIN Dietary Guidelines for Indians 2024, Guideline 9 "Points to register", PDF p. 85
Cut down consumption of sugar, salt, excess refined oil, deep fried foods and refined foods. Avoid soft drinks and alcohol. Increase consumption of fruits, vegetables and pulses. Prefer whole grains and millets to refined grains / ultra-processed foods.

## sleep-6-8-hours
source: ICMR-NIN Dietary Guidelines for Indians 2024, Guideline 9 "Points to register", PDF p. 85
Healthy sleeping patterns (6–8 hrs/day) is necessary to avoid unhealthy eating behaviors and resultant weight gain.

## screen-time
source: ICMR-NIN Dietary Guidelines for Indians 2024, Guideline 9 "Points to register", PDF p. 85
Minimize non-essential screen time.

## labels-read-them
source: ICMR-NIN Dietary Guidelines for Indians 2024, Guideline 17 "Points to register", PDF p. 127
Always read food label information to know about content of foods inside a pack. The ingredients are presented in a descending order, with the food item that has been used in highest quantity stated as the first item.

## labels-check-claims
source: ICMR-NIN Dietary Guidelines for Indians 2024, Guideline 17 "Points to register", PDF p. 127
Although nutrition and health claims are regulated by FSSAI, consumers are advised to exercise caution by cross-checking the claims with ingredients and nutrition information on the label.

## elderly-points-to-register
source: ICMR-NIN Dietary Guidelines for Indians 2024, Guideline 16 "Points to register", PDF p. 122
Eat a variety of nutrient-rich foods. Ensure regular physical activity. Avoid HFSS and UPF foods with high amounts of salt, sugar and fats. Consume adequate water to avoid dehydration.
