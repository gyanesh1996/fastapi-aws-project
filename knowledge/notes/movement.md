# Movement and exercise

## move-30-45-min-walk
source: ICMR-NIN Dietary Guidelines for Indians 2024, Guideline 10 "Points to register", PDF p. 89
A minimum 30–45 minutes brisk walk / physical activity of moderate intensity among adults helps in maintaining good health.

## move-benefits
source: ICMR-NIN Dietary Guidelines for Indians 2024, Guideline 10 "Points to register", PDF p. 89
Physical activity controls body weight, reduces fat mass, increases muscle mass and improves immune function. Being physically active can reduce the risk of chronic diseases such as diabetes, high blood pressure, heart disease, osteoporosis, arthritis and certain types of cancer. It creates a sense of wellbeing, increases confidence and avoids depression.

## move-yoga
source: ICMR-NIN Dietary Guidelines for Indians 2024, Guideline 10 "Points to register", PDF p. 89
Yoga is a holistic, structured and planned physical activity that also includes breathing exercises as well as physical and mental relaxation (meditation).

## move-busy-schedule-tips
source: ICMR-NIN Dietary Guidelines for Indians 2024, Guideline 10, PDF p. 88
At work, use a standing desk or stand every half hour. Walk for 5–10 minutes every few hours. Take all phone calls walking, both at work place or home. Use stairs instead of lifts/elevator. Park vehicle away from work place. While watching TV, move around every few minutes or during commercial breaks. As you get up in the morning, do some simple yoga and stretches to keep your muscles active and joints flexible.

## move-strength-training
source: ICMR-NIN Dietary Guidelines for Indians 2024, Guideline 10, PDF p. 88
Regular strength training with weights is necessary to maintain muscle mass and improve metabolism. Not using muscles causes muscle atrophy. Perform two to three weight-bearing exercise per week to delay age-related muscle loss.

## move-consult-doctor-first
source: ICMR-NIN Dietary Guidelines for Indians 2024, Guideline 10, PDF p. 88
People with illnesses or chronic diseases like heart disease, high blood pressure, diabetes, asthma, osteoporosis and obesity should consult a physician before taking up an exercise program. Men and women over 40 years of age should also consult a doctor or healthcare provider before starting a vigorous physical activity program.

## who-150-minutes
source: WHO fact sheet "Physical activity"
Nearly one third (31%) of the world's adult population, 1.8 billion adults, are physically inactive. That is, they do not meet the global recommendations of at least 150 minutes of moderate-intensity physical activity per week.

## who-any-activity-counts
source: WHO fact sheet "Physical activity"
The guidelines highlight that any amount of physical activity is better than none; all physical activity counts; all age groups should limit the amount of time being sedentary; and muscle strengthening benefits everyone.

## who-inactive-risk
source: WHO fact sheet "Physical activity"
People who are insufficiently active have a 20% to 30% increased risk of death compared to people who are sufficiently active.

## who-sitting-risks
source: WHO fact sheet "Physical activity"
Sedentary behaviour is any period of low-energy expenditure while awake such as sitting, reclining or lying. Lives are becoming increasingly sedentary through the use of motorized transport and the increased use of screens for work, education and recreation.
