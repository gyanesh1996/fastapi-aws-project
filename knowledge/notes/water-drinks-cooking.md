# Water, drinks and cooking

## water-eight-glasses
source: ICMR-NIN Dietary Guidelines for Indians 2024, Guideline 14, PDF p. 106
A normal healthy person needs to drink about eight glasses (approximately two litres) of water including beverages per day. During very hot weather and while undertaking vigorous physical activity, this requirement increases as a considerable amount of water is lost through sweat.

## water-safe-boiling
source: ICMR-NIN Dietary Guidelines for Indians 2024, Guideline 14, PDF p. 106
The simplest and efficient method of rendering water safe is straining and keeping the water boiling for 10–15 minutes. The boiling process kills all disease-causing organisms and also removes temporary hardness. However, boiling will not remove chemical impurities.

## drinks-points-to-register
source: ICMR-NIN Dietary Guidelines for Indians 2024, Guideline 14 "Points to register", PDF p. 109
Prefer butter milk, tender coconut water, lemon water etc., as beverages in hot weather. Avoid synthetic soft drinks and carbonated beverages. Synthetic soft drinks are not substitutes for water and therefore should be avoided. Avoid alcoholic beverages.

## drinks-coconut-water-caution
source: ICMR-NIN Dietary Guidelines for Indians 2024, Guideline 14, PDF p. 106
Tender coconut water is a good hydrating beverage which contains several minerals and provides 15 Kcal/ 100ml. However, in patients prone to hyperkalemia (in kidney and heart diseases), tender coconut water should be avoided.

## cooking-points-to-register
source: ICMR-NIN Dietary Guidelines for Indians 2024, Guideline 13 "Points to register", PDF p. 105
Do not wash vegetables and fruits after cutting or peeling. Do not soak the cut vegetables in water. Do not cook in excess water and discard it after cooking. Use only enough water for cooking. Always cover cooked foods with lids. Prefer pressure/steam cooking instead of deep frying/roasting. Consume more sprouted or fermented foods.

## cooking-no-reused-oil
source: ICMR-NIN Dietary Guidelines for Indians 2024, Guideline 13 "Points to register", PDF p. 105
Avoid reheating and reuse of left-over heated oils.
